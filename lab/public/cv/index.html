What I do

          

            
Versioning
One shoot, every format.
A master you already own becomes every format, market and duration, without rebuilding the set.

            
Product fidelity
The product stays untouched.
Shape, setting and stitching locked to the real piece in every version. Skin and colour finished by hand.

            
Provenance
AI declared, never hidden.
Deliveries built for Article 50 of the EU AI Act: AI use declared, provenance traceable, confidential by default.


            
Pipelines
Repeatable by design.
Every run prints its alignment, fidelity and processing time, so a result can be rerun and defended in review.

            
Post-production
Finished by hand.
Retouching and colour grading learned in luxury studios. Skin and fabric corrected by eye after the AI pass.

            
Compliance
Safe to ship.
AI use declared per asset under the EU AI Act, NDA-grade confidentiality and provenance that travels with the file.


            
Diagnosis
Why an image reads as fake.
Six measurable signatures, from identical detail in all three colour channels to flat grain. Prompting fixes none of them.

            
Tool building
Shipped and live.
Web apps on Vercel and Cloud Run that call Gemini and Replicate models, built with Claude Code under my direction.

            
Client side
Luxury brief to live demo.
I can sit with a brand committee and with engineers in the same week. Pitch-tested with an LVMH maison.
