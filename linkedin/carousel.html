Signature 01 · channels02 / 08

01

The same detail in all three colour channels.

In real skin, red light scatters under the surface, so the red channel carries less fine detail than the green. Generators make red, green and blue equally sharp, and the eye reads plastic with relief.

The fixGraft the detail per channel: red 0.62, green 1, blue 0.88.

Signature 02 · pores03 / 08

02

Pores spaced evenly across the face.

Real skin changes across the face: open pores on the T-zone, fine on the cheeks, vellus hair at the temples. Generated skin repeats one pattern everywhere.

The fixVary the texture gain with a slow noise field across the face.

Signature 03 · sharpness04 / 08

03

Sharp from corner to corner.

No real lens resolves the corners like the centre. A frame that is equally sharp everywhere reads as a render.

The fixFade the grafted detail radially toward the corners.

Signature 04 · optics05 / 08

04

Perfect optics.

Real lenses fringe colour on high-contrast edges and lose light in the corners. Generated images have neither.

The fixSub-pixel lateral aberration (red magnifies, blue contracts) and corner falloff.

Signature 05 · grain06 / 08

05

Flat grain.

Real grain peaks in the midtones and disappears in pure black and blown highlights. The blue channel grains more than the red.

The fixClustered grain, weighted by exposure and balanced per channel.

Signature 06 · highlights07 / 08

06

Highlights clipped flat.

Film rolls the highlights off gradually. Generated images often cut them dead at white.

The fixA film-style shoulder on the highlights.
